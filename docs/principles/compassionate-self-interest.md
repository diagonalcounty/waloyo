# Stance — compassionate self-interest

Issue: [#24](https://github.com/diagonalcounty/waloyo/issues/24).  
Related: Hayek [#20](https://github.com/diagonalcounty/waloyo/issues/20), evaluate Rand [#19](https://github.com/diagonalcounty/waloyo/issues/19).

The couple lives **inside** an economy, not above it. Townsfolk already trade. The player’s job is to add value — improve their own lives in a way that does not require someone else to lose.

---

## Three stances

These are player moves on the **same** opportunity, not three separate mini-games.

| Stance | Who is first | At whose expense | What the town gets |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selflessness | Others | Yourself | A gift this once; no lasting trade |
| Selfishness | You | Others | Less surplus, more fear |
| Compassionate self-interest | You, *through* a deal others will repeat | Nobody in the deal | A new good or service that stays |

**Selflessness** puts others first. **Selfishness** puts the self first by taking. **Compassionate self-interest** wants your way and refuses to take it out of someone else’s hide. It tries to enlarge the system the parties share.

Overlap with Rand: trade vs loot. Difference: this stance allows *care* as a reason to keep the system fair; Rand’s ethic is narrower (rational traders, no duty of compassion). Do not collapse the two on #19.

---

## Card: `stance-compassionate-self-interest`

**Name:** Get your way without making them pay for it.

**Source:** Project language (Waloyo). Kin to Smith’s butcher, Hayek’s price signals, Rand’s trader. Not a quotation.

**Mechanism:** Durable wealth is positive-sum. You keep showing up to a deal only if it was good for you; they only keep showing up if it was good for them. A household that finds a real gap (skill × shortage) raises its own life and the town’s at once.

**Player does:** Pick work the town is short of; sell at a price others will pay again; spend the gain on tools, not on blocking neighbors.

**Player fails:**
- *Selfless:* give the stock away. You are loved this week and gone from the market next week.
- *Selfish:* corner, coerce, or smash a rival’s stall. You eat well until nobody will deal with you — or until the road empties.

**Chapters:** open (needs other people already producing)

## What this asks of scale

The market-morning prototype already has *meet someone, decide*. It does not have a society. Scale means:

- Several households doing jobs whether you watch or not.
- A visible gap (no tanner, dear leather, cheap hides).
- Your self-interest is *which gap you fill*, not whether you click the kind option.

Stakes belong in the town (empty hook where boots should hang, your pot, their door) — not in another paragraph.
